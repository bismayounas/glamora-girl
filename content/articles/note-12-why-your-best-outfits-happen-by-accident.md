Everyone has those mornings: running late, grabbing whatever's clean, catching your reflection at noon and thinking, wait, this is good. It feels like luck, but it isn't. Rushed mornings strip away overthinking, and what's left is instinct — your actual taste, unfiltered.

Start paying attention to those accidents. When a thrown-together outfit works, take a photo before you change. After a month you'll have a small gallery of your own best work, and patterns will emerge: maybe you always reach for the same proportions, or one colour keeps showing up.

Then reverse-engineer it. Recreate the formula on purpose — same silhouette, same balance — and watch the "accident" become a signature. The goal was never to dress faster. It was to learn what your instincts already knew.
