Somewhere along the way we learned that one delicate necklace is "tasteful" and anything more is "too much." But look at the most stylish people you know — the ones with unmistakable personal style. They're rarely minimalists. They're pilers: rings stacked, scarves knotted over jackets, a watch worn next to three bracelets.

One accessory decorates an outfit. Three tell a story about who you are. The trick is keeping them in conversation with each other — mixed metals are fine, mixed moods are not. Gold hoops, a gold watch, a gold hair clip: that's a point of view.

Start small if the pile-up scares you. Add one more ring than usual. Then one more. Somewhere around three, you'll stop looking decorated and start looking like yourself — only more so.
