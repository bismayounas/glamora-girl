Clothes disappear from our wardrobes when the next step feels inconvenient. A loose button, a small mark or a knit that needs airing can send a good piece to the back of a chair for weeks.

Give yourself ten minutes before putting an outfit away. Hang delicate pieces where air can reach them, brush a coat, empty pockets and note any repair. Keep a small basket for mending so the job has a clear home instead of becoming visual clutter.

Care does not have to be precious. It is simply the habit that makes tomorrow's wardrobe feel full of possibilities rather than unfinished tasks.
