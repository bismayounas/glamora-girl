Jewellery feels most personal when every piece is not trying to match. Begin with the item you reach for without thinking—a slim chain, a watch, a ring with a story—then add one piece that differs in scale or finish.

Mixing metals works when something repeats. A gold clasp on a silver chain, or a two-tone watch beside separate bracelets, creates a bridge between the colours. Leave a little breathing room rather than filling every finger and neckline.

There is no need to complete the collection in one shopping trip. The charm comes from time: a gift, a market find, something borrowed and eventually returned. Let the mix grow at the speed of your life.
