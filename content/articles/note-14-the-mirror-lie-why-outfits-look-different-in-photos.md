The mirror is a flatterer and a liar. It shows you in reverse, in familiar bathroom light, from exactly the angle you've practiced since you were fourteen. No wonder an outfit that looked perfect at home feels off in every photo.

The camera, annoying as it is, shows what other people actually see: proportions, how things sit when you move, whether that top is really as flattering as the mirror claimed. So make the camera your fitting-room friend. Snap a quick photo before big days — you'll spot the gaping button, the bunching fabric, the hem that looked fine in reflection.

It stings at first. But the women with the most consistent style aren't luckier in front of mirrors — they just check the photo, adjust, and walk out knowing exactly how they look to the world.
