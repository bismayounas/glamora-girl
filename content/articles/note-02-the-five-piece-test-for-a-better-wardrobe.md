A beautiful piece can still be wrong for your wardrobe. Before taking it home, picture five things you already own that you would genuinely wear with it. Not the trousers you might buy next month or the imaginary coat in the perfect shade—the clothes that are hanging in your room today.

This little test reveals more than versatility. It shows whether the piece belongs to your actual life. A sequinned skirt may pass easily if you love dressing it down with knits and trainers; a supposedly sensible blouse may fail if its neckline never works with your favourite jackets.

Five is not a strict law. It is a pause between wanting and choosing. If the answer is only one outfit, decide whether that one outfit brings enough joy to earn the space.
