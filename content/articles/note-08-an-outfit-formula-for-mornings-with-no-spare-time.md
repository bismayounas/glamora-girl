A formula is not a uniform; it is a reliable starting point. Try a fitted base, a roomier bottom and one structured layer. The exact pieces can change—a fine knit, wide-leg trouser and blazer one day; a tee, full skirt and denim jacket the next.

Keep the final detail near the door. It might be a bright scarf, a pair of hoops or the lipstick that makes you feel awake. That small decision gives the outfit intention without asking for another ten minutes.

When a combination works, write it down or photograph it. Style becomes easier when you stop expecting inspiration to arrive before breakfast.
