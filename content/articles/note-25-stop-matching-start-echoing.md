Pink top, pink bag, pink shoes — and somehow the whole outfit looks cheaper than its parts. That's matching. Echoing is the opposite: one color that appears twice, never the same shade, never in the obvious place.

The trick: pick a color in your outfit and repeat it in a smaller, slightly different dose. Navy jeans + a tiny navy bead in your earring. White tee + off-white sneakers instead of optic white. Brown belt + tortoise sunglasses. The eye connects the dots, and the look feels pulled-together without looking planned.

Matching says "I tried." Echoing says "I have taste." Big difference — zero extra cost.

The practice: before you leave the house, find one color echo in your outfit. Not two identical items — one whisper of a repeat. Do it for a week and watch how much more "styled" your photos start looking.
