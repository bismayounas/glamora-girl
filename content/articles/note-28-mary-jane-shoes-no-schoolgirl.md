Mary Jane shoes just hit a 5-year high in searches, and they come with one fear attached: the schoolgirl look.

The strap is the whole personality of the shoe — so the trick is making everything around it read adult. First rule: skip the knee socks. Bare legs or sheer tights keep the shoe in grown-up territory instantly. Second: let the hemline do the counterbalancing. A midi skirt or wide-leg trouser against the strap reads deliberate; a short pleated skirt against the strap reads costume.

Color and texture matter more than the shoe's shape. Patent black screams uniforms; suede, chocolate brown, or a soft metallic reads evening. A pointed or almond toe (over the round one) sharpens the whole thing — the same logic that makes pointed ballet flats read sharper.

Mary Jane outfit ideas that work right now: chocolate suede Mary Janes with cropped wide-leg trousers and a cream knit (quiet luxury, zero effort), or black almond-toe Mary Janes with a lace midi skirt for dinner.

The practice: wear your Mary Janes with the most "serious" thing you own — tailored trousers, a long coat, a structured bag. If the strap still reads schoolgirl against all that, it's the shoe. It won't.
