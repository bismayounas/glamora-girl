Nobody talks about this, but lighting is the secret stylist of every outfit. That silk blouse that glows in daylight can look washed out under office fluorescents. Matte black looks razor-sharp at noon and disappears entirely in a dim restaurant.

Before you choose, ask where you'll actually be seen. Daylight loves texture and true colour — wear the rust, the emerald, the interesting weave. Evening forgives a lot but rewards shine: a little satin, a metallic shoe, something that catches candlelight.

And the harshest critic of all is the office ceiling light. If you work under fluorescents, favour structure over subtlety — crisp lines and deliberate colour read better than delicate details that the lighting will swallow whole.
