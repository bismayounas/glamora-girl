The best trends feel strangely familiar. Maybe everyone is suddenly wearing burgundy, but you have always loved deep, moody colour. Perhaps full skirts are everywhere and the shape reminds you of a dress you wore until it fell apart. That sense of recognition matters more than hype.

Try the trend in the smallest possible way before committing. Borrow the proportion with a piece you own, test the colour near your face, or save three outfits you would honestly repeat. If you like only the idea of it—but cannot picture Tuesday morning in it—that is useful information.

You do not need to be early, and you do not need to participate. A trend is worthwhile when it gives language to something already present in your style, not when it asks you to replace yourself.
