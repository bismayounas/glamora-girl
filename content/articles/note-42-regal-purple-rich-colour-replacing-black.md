Regal purple is up +60% this winter, and the appeal is easy to see: the rich colour replacing black, a deep plum that does everything black does — slims, sharpens, goes with everything — while looking like you actually chose something. Black disappears into the background; regal purple holds the room. A plum coat, a violet knit, a purple skirt against winter neutrals: no loud print, no extra styling, just one rich colour that reads like an outfit before you have added anything else. It is black's more interesting cousin, made for days when you want to look deliberate without trying hard.

The rule is what keeps it expensive-looking instead of loud. Let the purple be the one rich thing and keep everything around it quiet: chocolate brown or camel underneath, cream or black at the edges, never another bright colour fighting it. Keep the base simple and structured — straight-leg trousers, a long wool skirt, tall boots — and let the colour be the statement. Pair it with gold, never silver, and keep jewellery to one piece: a single gold cuff is all it needs. Stack a second bold colour on top and the look turns loud; keep the rest calm and the purple reads like quiet luxury, not a costume.

It earns its keep across the wardrobe. In a deep plum it is rich enough for an evening out without trying too hard, while a softer violet knit keeps it wearable for daytime — the same colour, two different moods. An oversized blazer in charcoal over a purple top tames it for work, and a lace bandana in burgundy keeps the richness from feeling too severe. One rich colour, one quiet base, and winter's boldest neutral does the work for you: regal, wearable, and never loud.
