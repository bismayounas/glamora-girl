We've all been told to invest in basics: the white tee, the black trouser, the plain sweater. But basics are forgettable by design, and a closet full of them still leaves you staring blankly each morning. What you actually need are anchors — pieces with enough character to build an outfit around.

An anchor is the thing people remember: the perfectly cut blazer, the boots with attitude, the coat that makes everything underneath it look deliberate. You need fewer of them than you think. Three strong anchors can carry thirty outfits.

Next time you shop, ask one question: can I build three different outfits around this? If yes, it's an anchor. If it just "goes with everything," it's a basic — and you probably own it already.
