Most of us dress for the day we have. Meetings, errands, the weather forecast. But your clothes are the fastest mood-edit you own — and they kick in before the coffee does.

Want to feel braver? Wear the piece you've been saving for "someday." Someday is a storage unit, not a calendar. That jacket, those heels, the red lipstick from March — put them on a Tuesday. The bravery follows the outfit, not the other way around.

Want calm? Soften everything: loose silhouettes, muted tones, nothing that pinches. Comfort isn't laziness; it's a signal to your nervous system that nothing is urgent.

Want energy? One bold thing is enough. A bright bag, a patterned scarf, shoes that click when you walk. Energy doesn't need a full costume — it needs one exclamation point.

The rule: pick the mood first, the outfit second. Two minutes in the morning, and the mirror starts working for you instead of against you.
