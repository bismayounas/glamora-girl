By the time a trend reaches the high street, stylists are already bored of it. Their trick isn't prediction — it's early adoption in small doses. They spot a silhouette or colour gaining quiet momentum and wear a whisper of it months before it becomes a shout.

You can do the same without a fashion-week invitation. Notice what keeps appearing in your feed: a particular bag shape, a colour that won't leave, a way of styling a scarf. Don't buy the full trend. Buy one small, cheap version and wear it now.

If it fizzles, you've lost nothing. If it explodes six months later, you're the friend who "was wearing that ages ago." Being early beats being fashionable — and it costs almost nothing to try.
