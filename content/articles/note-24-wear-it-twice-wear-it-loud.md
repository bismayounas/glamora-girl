Nobody remembers what you wore on Tuesday. Repeat it on Friday and suddenly it's your thing.

Repeating outfits has a bad reputation because we do it apologetically — hiding the same top under a different jacket, hoping nobody notices. Do the opposite. Wear the same look twice in a row and style it better the second time. Now it looks intentional, and intentional always reads expensive.

This is how personal style actually forms. It isn't one new outfit a day — it's a handful of formulas you return to, slightly refined. The woman with the "effortless style" you admire? She's wearing her greatest hits.

The practice: pick one outfit you love this week and wear it twice. Different day, different hair, one accessory swapped. Keep a phone note of your three best formulas. When you're in a rush, don't shop your closet — run your hits.

The goal isn't more clothes. It's more you, more often.
