Sometimes a wardrobe stops feeling like you because you have changed faster than your clothes. A new routine, climate, job or season of life can shift what comfort and confidence look like. That is not a style failure; it is evidence that you are paying attention.

Before clearing everything out, identify what still works. Maybe you still love clean lines but need softer fabrics, or bold colour still delights you while high-maintenance pieces do not. Keep the thread and change the expression.

Try one small experiment each week and notice what you reach for again. Personal style is not a final answer. It is an ongoing conversation between taste, body, memory and the day in front of you.
