Jeans shopping becomes easier when you treat fit as useful information instead of a verdict. Sizes vary wildly, so start with how the denim feels. Rigid fabric holds a shape and softens over time; stretch feels forgiving straight away but may relax after a few wears.

Next, test the rise while sitting, not just standing. Walk, bend and use the pockets. The pair should support the way you move through an ordinary day. If the waist gaps or the fabric pulls, that cut is not doing its job.

Bring two shoes you wear often and ignore the number on the label. The right jeans are the pair you stop thinking about once they are on.
