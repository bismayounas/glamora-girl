Here's a secret from people who always look polished: before leaving, they pause for three seconds and fix exactly three things. Smooth one wrinkle, tuck one corner, straighten one strap. That's it. The eye reads those small acts of intention as "expensive," even when the whole outfit came from the sale rack.

Price shows in fabric and fit, but polish shows in maintenance. A steamed twenty-dollar shirt beats a wrinkled two-hundred-dollar one every single time. Keep a lint roller by the door, learn the half-tuck, and never underestimate what clean shoes do for an entire look.

Try it tomorrow. Three seconds, three fixes, then walk out without a second thought. You'll be amazed how often strangers assume the whole outfit cost triple.
