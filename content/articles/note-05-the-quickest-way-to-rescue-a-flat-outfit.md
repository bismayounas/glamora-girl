Most flat outfits do not need another accessory; they need tension. If everything is loose, define one line. If every piece is dark, bring in a pale bag or a glimpse of white sock. If the look feels very polished, try a worn-in jacket or a relaxed shoe.

Contrast can also come from scale. A small fitted top changes the energy of a generous trouser, while a bold earring wakes up a simple neckline. Choose one adjustment and give it room to work.

Take a quick photo before adding anything else. The camera often makes the missing balance easier to see—and stops you from turning a good outfit into a crowded one.
