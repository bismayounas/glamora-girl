Every interesting outfit has one decision that feels slightly wrong. Nine times out of ten, it's the shoe. The dress wants heels; you wear boots. The suit wants loafers; you wear sneakers. That small friction is what turns "nice outfit" into "wait, who is she."

Matching shoes to the mood of an outfit is safe, and safe is forgettable. A feminine dress with chunky boots, tailoring with trainers, evening silk with flat sandals — the contrast creates tension, and tension is memorable.

So next time an outfit feels almost there but boring, don't change the clothes. Change the shoe to the one that feels wrong. Then look again. The "mistake" was the missing ingredient all along.
