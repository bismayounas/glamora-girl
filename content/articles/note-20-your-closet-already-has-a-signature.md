Finding "your style" sounds like a quest, but it's actually an audit. Open your closet and look for the evidence: which pieces do you reach for on good days? What's the one thing you'd rescue in a fire? Your signature isn't aspirational — it's behavioural.

Lay your five most-worn pieces on the bed. Ignore what you think they say and look at what they share: a neckline that repeats, a preferred length, a colour family, a level of polish. That's your signature, already formed, waiting to be noticed.

Once you see it, dressing gets easier overnight. Buy more of what the evidence supports, stop buying what it doesn't, and let the aspirational pieces go to someone whose signature they actually match. Your style was never missing. You just hadn't read the data.
